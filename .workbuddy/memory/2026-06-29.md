- Story dialogue cards are `once: true` — they appear once and carry weighty moral/emotional choices with arc-advancing tags
- World atmosphere cards are repeatable (no `once`) — lightweight ambient cards that make the world feel alive without major choices
- All cards use `type: "STORY"` to pass through `_drawNormal()` filters
